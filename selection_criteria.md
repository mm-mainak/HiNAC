# Catalog selection — summary of manuscript Section 3

HiNAC is a curated set of non-blazar AGNs intended for neutrino-source candidate searches. We begin with the 70-month BASS catalog, supplement with the Swift/BAT 157-month catalog, dedicated torus-model fits of heavily obscured BASS AGNs, and NuSTAR XClumpy analyses of candidate Compton-thick AGNs. The catalogs are cross-matched, and adopted X-ray measurements are homogenized to intrinsic 2–10 keV and 2–195 keV fluxes.

The manuscript models the intrinsic X-ray spectrum as a cutoff power law, adopts literature absorption-corrected luminosities where appropriate, and ranks sources independently in both bands. A broadband flux threshold of F_X,intr(2–195 keV) >= 1e-7 GeV cm^-2 s^-1 reduces the candidate pool to 39 before source-specific selection and validation lead to the final 28 AGNs. See Section 3 and its source-specific notes for detailed inclusion/exclusion decisions; this summary is not a substitute for those decisions.

The catalog is independent of the specific neutrino emission model used later in the manuscript for forecast calculations. The sky tags use declination: N for >= +5 deg, S for <= -5 deg, H for |declination| < 5 deg.
